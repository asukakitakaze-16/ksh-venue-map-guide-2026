About The Event

About the Kyushu–Okinawa
Hospitality & Food Service Industry Expo &
Asia Export Food Expo

Hospitality & Food Week in Kyushu–Okinawa is the region’s largest business event, featuring two exhibitions:

The Kyushu–Okinawa Hospitality & Food Service Industry Expo

Showcasing the latest solutions for the accommodation and food service sectors.

The Asia Export Food Expo

Supporting the global expansion of exceptional regional food products.

Organized by the Japan Management Association (JMA)—well-known for FOODEX JAPAN and HCJ—the event leverages decades of expertise to drive regional economic growth and meet strong demand for overseas market development.

The exhibition will be held on October 28–29, 2026 at Marine Messe Fukuoka, with approximately 200 exhibitors and 6,000 visitors expected.

Join us for a prime opportunity to expand your business from the region to the global market.

Meeting Program

Maximize your results through both exhibition and business meetings

The organizer invites key buyers from Japan—particularly those in the Kyushu hospitality and food service sectors—and from overseas, including importers and retailers mainly across Asia.

Exhibitors and buyers are matched in advance, ensuring confirmed business meetings take place during the event. This effective program supports not only global market expansion but also new business development within the Kyushu region.
